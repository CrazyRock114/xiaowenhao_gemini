⚡ 神经元：放电的 IF 语句The Neuron: an IF-statement that fires

海报左上角：一根神经元的解剖Top-left of the poster: anatomy of one neuron

  • 🌳 树突 Dendrites — 接收 thousands of inputs；海报在此标注“往里看 Look Inside：树突 / 神经元”。collect thousands of inputs; the poster tags a "Look Inside: Dendrite / Neuron" magnifier here.
  • 🧵 轴突 Axons — 把输出信号送往下一站，海报用“导线”来比喻。carry the output onward — the poster's wiring.
  • 🔋 静息电位 Resting potential：−70 mV — 神经元待机时的膜电位。the neuron's idling membrane voltage.
  • 🧪 离子与递质 Ions & neurotransmitters — Na⁺ · K⁺ · Ca²⁺；多巴胺 Dopamine、谷氨酸 Glutamate（兴奋），GABA（抑制），血清素 Serotonin（调制）。dopamine and glutamate (excitatory), GABA (inhibitory), serotonin (modulatory).
IF ( D⁺ + G⁺ − GABA⁻ ) + Others > +20 mV  THEN  FIRE ⚡

动作电位（action potential）：当兴奋输入（多巴胺 D、谷氨酸 G）减去抑制输入（GABA），再加上其他输入，使膜电位越过 +20 mV 阈值，神经元就“放电”。下面自己动手试：Action potential: when excitation (dopamine D, glutamate G) minus inhibition (GABA), plus other inputs, pushes the membrane past +20 mV, the neuron fires. Try it yourself below.

🎯 跟随 8 大感觉输入Follow the 8 Sensory Inputs

海报用编号 1–8 标出八条进入大脑的信号通路。每张卡片汇总了海报上该通路的全部数字与标注。The poster numbers eight input channels, 1–8. Each card gathers every number and label the poster gives that channel.

1. 👁️ 视觉Sight

  • 视网膜里的视觉处理：1.25 亿个视杆细胞 + 600 万个彩色视锥细胞 → 压缩成视神经中仅 100 万条轴突（单眼标注 1.26 亿视杆）。Visual processing in the retina: 125 million rods & 6 million color cone photoreceptors are compressed to just 1 million axons in the optic nerve (the eye diagram labels 126 million rods).
  • 视网膜会压缩“视频”、检测运动、留意“事故征兆”、对碰撞路径上的物体发出警报，并自动向四肢发送反射控制信号——估算每只眼超过 4 颗 Apple A9 处理器的处理能力。The retina compresses video, detects movement and accident evidence, watches for objects on a collision path, and automatically sends reflex control to the limbs — an estimated 4+ Apple A9 processors per eye.
  • 中央凹 Fovea（“小凹坑”）：直径约 1.5 mm，聚集 20 万个视锥细胞——高分辨率视野。Fovea ("the pit"): ~1.5 mm across, holding 200,000 cones — the foveal high-resolution view.
  • 视网膜厚度约 0.12 mm，总面积约 2,500 mm²。Retinal thickness ~0.12 mm; total retinal area ~2,500 mm².
  • 周边视野 = 低分辨率，但周边运动感知电路持续提供视觉反射：运动检测中央凹反射 + 平滑追踪中央凹反射。The periphery is low-resolution, but its motion-sensing circuitry drives visual reflexes: motion-detection and smooth-pursuit foveal reflexes.
  • 视神经 100 万连接 → 视觉中继 → 视辐射 570K 条纤维 → 枕叶 V1。动眼神经控制眼肌；海报同时画出左眼与右眼。Optic nerve: 1 million connections → vision relay → optic radiations: 570K fibers → V1 in the occipital lobe. The oculomotor nerve steers the eye muscles; both eyes are drawn in the poster.

2. 👃 嗅觉Smell

  • 12,000,000 个嗅觉感受器细胞（人类 Human）。olfactory receptor cells (human).
  • 1,000,000,000 个（狗 Dog）。(dog).
  • 5,000,000,000 个（猎犬 Hound）——比人类多 400 多倍。(hound) — over 400× a human's.
  • 海报中“气味 Odors”的箭头绕过丘脑，直达情绪与奖赏回路——这就是气味为何能瞬间唤起记忆与欲望。In the poster, the "Odors" arrows bypass the thalamus and land straight on the emotion and reward circuits — why a smell can trigger memory and craving instantly.

3. 👅 味觉Taste

  • 9,000 个味蕾。taste buds.
  • 每个味蕾含 5–10 个感受器细胞。Each bud holds 5–10 receptor cells.
  • 味觉信号上传至岛叶皮层——海报在岛叶上同时标注“味觉 Taste”与“性 Sexual”。Taste ascends to the insular cortex — where the poster also tags "Sexual".

4. ✋ 触觉Touch

“你皮肤的每一平方厘米约有 200 个痛觉感受器，而压力感受器只有 15 个，冷觉 6 个，温觉 2 个。”

"Every square centimetre of your skin contains around 200 pain receptors but only 15 receptors for pressure, 6 for cold and 2 for warmth."

— BBC Science & Nature
  • 触觉沿“痛觉-触觉-本体感觉”通路上行至顶叶体感区（海报标注 Pain–Touch–Proprioception → Sensory Area）。Touch ascends via the pain–touch–proprioception line to the parietal sensory area.

5. 👂 听觉Hearing

  • 15,500 个毛细胞在耳蜗（Cochlea）里。hair cells in the cochlea.
  • 30,000 条连接在听神经（Auditory Nerve）中。connections in the auditory nerve.
  • 100,000,000 个神经元在听觉皮层（Auditory Cortex）。neurons in the auditory cortex.
  • 信号经声音中继 Sound Relay（丘脑）送往颞叶，皮层在此完成词语 Words 处理；海报还标出视听反射 Visual/Audio Reflexes。Signals pass the sound relay (thalamus) to the temporal lobe for words; the poster also marks visual/audio reflexes.

6. 🤸 平衡与加速度Balance & Acceleration

  • 前庭 Vestibule 接收“陀螺仪 + 加速度计”输入——即军用加速度计-陀螺三件套（Military Accelerometer-Gyro Triad）。The vestibule takes gyro & accelerometer inputs — a military accelerometer-gyro triad.
  • “前庭器类似于军用制导系统和航天飞机上使用的主传感器。”（海报原文）"Vestibule is similar to the main sensor used in Military Guidance and the Space Shuttle." (poster text)
  • 三大功能（海报剪影）：图像稳定 Image Stabilization · 导航 Navigation · 眼反射 Eye Reflex；另有“图像稳定对抗反射（平衡）” Image-Stabilization Counter Reflex。Three poster functions: image stabilization, navigation, eye reflex — plus the image-stabilization counter reflex for balance.
  • 小脑深处藏着惯性导航计算机：“研究者发现，小脑深处埋着一台精密的神经计算机，它执行惯性导航计算，解算一个人在空间中的运动。”——《大脑惯性导航系统被定位》Deep in the cerebellum sits an inertial-navigation computer: "Researchers have discovered a sophisticated neural computer, buried deep in the cerebellum, that performs inertial navigation calculations to figure out a person's movement through space." — Brain's Inertial Navigation System Pinpointed
  • 与视觉追踪联动：“头部运动时，两套系统必须与前庭系统互动。追踪系统的目标是让眼速匹配目标速度，使目标图像稳定落在中央凹上。”——前庭相关额叶皮层与平滑追踪Linked with pursuit: "During head movements, both systems must interact with the vestibular system. The goal of the pursuit system is to keep the retinal target image on the fovea by matching the eye velocity to target velocity." — The vestibular-related frontal cortex and its role in smooth-pursuit

7. 🦴 关节位置（本体感觉）Joint Position / Proprioception

  • 闭上眼也知道手脚在哪——肌腱与关节的本体感觉持续上报；小脑据此设置力量限制 Strength Limits，防止你把自己拧断。You know where your limbs are with eyes shut — proprioception reports continuously, and the cerebellum uses it to set strength limits so you don't wrench yourself apart.
  • 信息沿脊髓上传：脊髓有 10 亿个神经元，灰质在横截面上呈蝴蝶形（脊髓板层 lamina）；髓鞘绝缘的轴突（“导线”）沿脊髓上下行。It rides the spinal cord: 1 billion neurons, gray matter shaped like a butterfly (the spinal laminae), with myelin-insulated axon "wiring" running up and down.
  • “人类运动神经元最长的轴突可以超过 1 米。”（海报标注）"The largest axon of a human motor neuron can be over a meter long." (poster note)
  • 海报还标出“手臂层 Arm Layer”与“中间神经元 Inter Neurons”——“脊髓中必然存在复杂的神经元间回路，才能在双侧多个部位协调肢体伸肌与屈肌活动。中间神经元最多可占脊髓全部细胞的 97%。”——《脊髓》Marieb《人体解剖生理学》The poster's "Arm Layer" and "Inter Neurons" tags: "A complex inter-neuronal circuitry must exist to coordinate the control of limb extensor and flexor activity across multiple points bilaterally. Interneurons make up as much as 97% of all cells in the cord." — Spinal cord, Marieb, Human A&P
